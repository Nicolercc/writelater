You are now improving the existing WriteLater frontend shell. Treat the current UI as a rough prototype, not final product design.

Current problem:
The existing Capture screen is too plain, too empty, and too generic. It looks like a basic form inside a phone frame. It does not yet communicate the core product wedge: this app is a temporary mobile brain for people who love physical planners and want to transfer captured thoughts into their paper planner later.

Design goal:
Make the product feel like:
- a calm premium mobile app
- a soft paper planner companion
- a fast capture tool
- a thoughtful staging area before writing in a physical planner
- beautiful enough to screenshot
- emotionally gentle, not corporate
- more crafted than a CRUD app

Important:
Do not make it cluttered.
Do not make it childish.
Do not make it look like a generic todo app.
Do not overuse gradients.
Do not add random features.
Do not use harsh black text everywhere.
Do not let chips overflow awkwardly.
